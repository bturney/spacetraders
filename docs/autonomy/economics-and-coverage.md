# Economic operation, Marketplace coverage and Neutral Wait

> Imported from [#502](https://github.com/bturney/spacetraders/issues/502) on 2026-10-18. The contract text below is preserved from that issue without semantic edits. Qualification status is tracked separately; a written contract is not proof of implementation.

### C05 — Useful, adaptive, cost-aware operation

Contribution comparison is objective-specific and dimensionally meaningful. Declared expected benefit, uncertainty, Ship time, travel/fuel, capacity, capital, market depth and price movement, acquisition/preparation, switching, and safe-unwind cost must be available to the deciding module. Raw distance, arbitrary observation scores, and credits are not interchangeable measures.

Market trading reconciles source evidence before purchase and destination evidence before sale or another admissible disposition. Market roles, supply/activity, and tradeVolume are retained when returned. Realized price, credits, Cargo, fuel, time, and transaction evidence qualify estimates. Credit protection uses defensible consequence bounds, not constants asserted to be worst case. When the API cannot enforce the desired guarantee, the Strategy review and admission result explain that limitation truthfully.

Information gathering and preparation earn their place by unlocking or improving declared outcomes. No unconditional mining, continuous low-value scouting, or keep-busy fallback exists. Acquisition competes on marginal Strategy value and preparation/payback exposure while other work is current. Contracts and shared outcomes follow declared priorities, current eligibility, deadlines, exposure, and total cost, rather than a blanket source-derived ranking.

Ordinary switching requires material expected improvement after uncertainty, transition, and unwind costs; safety or authoritative invalidation may preempt. Parameters are explicit, versioned, and bounded, with conservative initial models. Economic adaptation remains within the active revision. A mandatory Game Phase controller, automatic preset switching, or speculative late-game preset is not a prerequisite to completing the original destination.

### C06 — Bounded coverage, Neutral Wait, and real stalls

Retain [Decide the bounded Marketplace coverage contribution contract](https://github.com/bturney/spacetraders/issues/482), including its amendment: the target grows with authoritative Marketplace discoveries in **every discovered System**, starting in the headquarters System after reset. One demand per subject remains the durable coverage representation; there is no second truth-maintenance ledger for the target set.

A Coverage Contribution names a finite set in fixed planner order and revalidates one next observation at a time. Each completed observation is an allocation boundary. A newly supported worthwhile contribution may supersede coverage there; remaining demands stay open. Partial coverage may prove a positive opportunity. A negative conclusion requires every relevant subject fulfilled or proven objectively infeasible. Temporarily unreachable subjects remain unresolved and may justify capability acquisition.

Only Fleet Allocation can establish Neutral Wait: no worthwhile admissible contribution currently exists and re-evaluation has a durable future observation time. Equivalent waits retain one episode per Operator/Generation/revision scope; a changed limitation, selected plan, revision, or Generation supersedes it. Unknown availability, capacity deferral, a malfunctioning executor, and objective infeasibility cannot masquerade as Neutral Wait.

| Disposition | Meaning and Operator treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Progressing or normal game wait | An admissible outcome is progressing or awaiting a declared game event; show purpose and expected continuation. |
| Neutral Wait | Deliberate allocation result with limitation and future evidence time; no Attention. |
| Evidence unavailable or capacity deferred | State what is missing or deferred, retain due work, and show recovery/re-evaluation timing; not a fabricated zero or Neutral Wait. |
| Objective infeasible | Authoritative evidence disproves the objective within permitted resources/constraints; Attention, with independent work continuing. |
| Degraded Operation or broken progress | A capability/fault materially impairs Strategy, or execution violates its declared continuation/recovery expectation; durable Attention with causal evidence and autonomous recovery. |
| Intervention | The accepted external-authority, contradictory-Strategy, irreconcilable-safety, or failed verified durable-recovery boundary is crossed; name the required Operator action. |

A progress monitor evaluates actual commitment progress, due work, expected outcomes, recovery obligations, and capable time. It does not simply count process uptime, completed observations, empty portfolios, or minutes without credit growth. Healthy deliberate waits do not alarm; a Fleet stuck on an unrecorded action or perpetually rediscovering the same facts cannot remain silently healthy. Acknowledgement and resolution are distinct durable facts.
